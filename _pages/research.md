<div class="page-intro nomad-panel">
  <p class="micro-label">Research philosophy</p>
  <p class="page-intro__lead">Benchmarks are snapshots. We are interested in what happens after the data distribution, environment, or decision objective starts to move.</p>
  <p>Our research routes overlap deliberately: perception needs uncertainty, agents need reliable evidence, and scientific models need both structure and adaptability.</p>
</div>

  <div class="principle-grid">
    <div><strong>01</strong><h3>Start from the decision</h3><p>Evaluation should reflect what a model is expected to change, not only what is easy to measure.</p></div>
    <div><strong>02</strong><h3>Stress the boundary</h3><p>We test models under shift, scarcity, ambiguity, and uncertainty rather than only on familiar samples.</p></div>
    <div><strong>03</strong><h3>Keep evidence visible</h3><p>Methods should expose confidence, provenance, and failure modes wherever possible.</p></div>
